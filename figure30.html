Figure 3.2: Risso's dolphin buzz

Figure 3.2: An example of a buzz following a regular click train produced by a Risso’s dolphin. An ICI threshold of <16 ms (horizontal line) was used to identify buzzes from that of regular echolocation clicks (Arranz et al., 2016). ICIs of detected clicks can be seen in the top plot, within ICIs and recieved levels gradually reducing until the buzz call.
