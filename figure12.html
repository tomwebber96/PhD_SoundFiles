Figure 2.3: Spectral Banding

Figure 2.3: Examples of echolocation click trains from a Risso’s dolphin collected in Scottish waters (top - slowed by 0.5), white-beaked dolphin collected in English waters (middle - slowed by 0.5), and common dolphins collected in Scottish waters (C). While individual click energy can be seen originating at 20 kHz extending up to the 48 kHz limit of the spectrogram in all three species, clear energy peaks can be seen in both spectrograms for Risso’s and white-beaked dolphins. Across a click train for these two species, these consistent energy peaks present as horizontal bands leading to description of these clicks being spectrally banded. Time and frequency resolution of spectrograms 10.67 ms and 93.75 Hz respectively from a 2048 FFT with 50% overlap.
