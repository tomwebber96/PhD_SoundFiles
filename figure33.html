Figure 4.2 - High frequency click trains from RD and WBD

Figure 4.2: Full bandwidth examples of echolocation click trains from a Risso’s dolphin collected in Scottish waters (top - slowed by 0.5) and white-beaked dolphin collected in English waters (bottom). Differences in the frequency content of echolocation clicks between the two species can be seen with white-beaked dolphins typically conatining more energy at higher frequencies. Differences in the banding pattern can also be seen with Risso’s dolphins containing more dense banding compared to white-beaked dolphins. Time and frequency resolution of spectrogram 1.78 ms and 140.6 Hz respectively from an FFT of 4,096 and 75% overlap..
