Figure 2.4: Spectrogram showing the full bandwidth of Risso’s dolphin echolocation clicks. Individual echolocation clicks are broadband from aproximtley 20 kHz extending up to 200 kHz. Peak energy is 45 kHz with spectral banding (SB) clearly evident between 20 and 50 kHz. Time and frequency resolution of spectrogram 1.78 ms and 140.6 Hz respectively from an FFT of 4,096 and 75% overlap. Slowed by 0.5.
